# Product Requirements

This document is the index of the product requirements. It holds no requirement text. The requirements are in the files under `docs/product/`, which state what the product does.

## Citation Convention

`PRODUCT.md §N` is the stable identifier of a requirement section. A section is its number together with all its numbered subsections, wherever they are stored. A section number never changes and is never reused. A new section takes the next free number under its parent. A heading title may change; the number is the identifier. A phrase such as "in this section" therefore names the whole numbered section, in whatever files its subsections are stored.

Tests cite a section with `// @requirement PRODUCT.md §N`. `PRODUCT.md` in that marker names the specification, not one file.
