# Testing Signal and Feedback Initiative

## Objective and authorization

Assess whether the test suite and validation workflow find meaningful defects promptly without making routine changes unnecessarily slow. The Product Owner requested analysis and a durable plan on 2026-09-28, and explicitly requested **no implementation changes in that session**. This plan records proposed follow-up work; no test, runtime, threshold, or validation-policy change is authorized for the planning session. A later request to continue this initiative authorizes its next ready task within the boundaries below, under [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) §8.

Keep current product behavior and mandatory validation gates until evidence supports a specific adjustment. Do not raise global coverage floors merely to improve a percentage, remove boundary or visual checks, refactor renderer state without a demonstrated cause, or change the technology or persistence model. The source requirements are [PRODUCT.md](../docs/PRODUCT.md) §§2.1, 2.4, and 22, [ARCHITECTURE.md](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) §§9–10, and [DEVELOPMENT.md](../docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) §§8–12.

## Findings that shape the work

- At `4f3f365` with a clean worktree, `/usr/bin/time -p npm run check` passed in 13.90 seconds on the development machine. Its coverage stage passed 72 files and 1,045 tests in 4.45 seconds. V8 unit/component coverage was 93.57% statements, 87.24% branches, 94.21% functions, and 96.17% lines, above the configured global floors of 91/83/92/93. The report does not include Electron end-to-end execution. These timings are one local observation, not a workflow baseline.
- The last 50 commits contain a large inline-expansion feature spanning domain, application, renderer, Electron tests, and performance tests, alongside many Vim interaction fixes and ownership refactors. Inline expansion already has focused and property tests, 13 Electron scenarios, and a scale guard. Its pure `visible-tree.ts` and `expansion-state.ts` modules have complete unit branch coverage. The two subsequent alignment fixes concerned real-renderer geometry and focus appearance; a higher unit branch floor would not have detected those pixel relationships. The new geometry and screenshot checks now guard them.
- [PRODUCT.md](../docs/PRODUCT.md) §2.4 says reopening the application starts with nodes collapsed. The expansion Electron spec covers entering and leaving locations but has no explicit expand–quit–relaunch assertion, and a repository-wide search found no other expansion/reopen case. This is a specific process-boundary coverage gap to verify and close. It is not evidence of a known implementation defect.
- `NodeList.tsx` has about 79.8% unit branch coverage and `use-node-list-drag.ts` about 81.1%, but many existing unit and Electron cases already exercise windowing, focus, drag, and expansion. The uncovered branches need semantic inspection before deciding whether another test is valuable.
- `npm run check:full` adds real Electron E2E and serial visible performance suites to `npm run check`. [ADR 0012](../docs/decisions/0012-parallel-e2e-execution.md) measured a roughly 55-second hidden E2E run after parallelization, but that is historical and does not measure the current full gate. [DEVELOPMENT.md](../docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) §10 already has a blank three-product-outcome workflow-speed pilot. Use it rather than creating a second timing policy. The expansion outcome occurred after that pilot was introduced but was not entered; do not invent retrospective timings. Record three fully observed future outcomes, and explicitly check pilot recording at each handoff.

## Decisions and boundaries for future sessions

The present recommendation is **targeted coverage and measured feedback**, with existing thresholds retained. TSF-1 and TSF-2 may add tests for existing requirements. TSF-3 gathers evidence through ordinary authorized product work; this plan does not itself authorize those product changes. TSF-4 may clarify focused commands within existing gates. A proposal to lower or remove a mandatory gate, change CI platform coverage, accept a confidence tradeoff, or alter user-visible behavior requires a Product Owner decision before implementation. A runtime defect found during TSF-1 or TSF-2 must be scoped and validated as its own fix; do not bury it in test-maintenance work.
